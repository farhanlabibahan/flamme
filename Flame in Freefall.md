## What the experiments measured

### Do liquid-fuel flames put themselves out?

Share of FLEX droplet flames that went out before the fuel was used up, by oxygen level (ISS, nitrogen-diluted air)

### Materials burn at lower oxygen in orbit

Lowest oxygen at which acrylic rods still burned, BASS-II on ISS vs the same test on Earth

### How long until the smoke alarm?

Saffire IV-VI cabin-scale fires on Cygnus: time for a detector across the cabin to reach 2 mg/m³ of smoke

### Carbon monoxide in the cabin

Same seven fires: peak rise in cabin-wide CO, and the CO already present when the alarm sounded, against NASA crew limits

Sources: NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (psi.nasa.gov) test tables and NASA Technical Reports Server (ntrs.nasa.gov). Every finding links to its source and page. Severity ratings and the ranking formula are the team's own judgement.
