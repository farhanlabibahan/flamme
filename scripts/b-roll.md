## Editing tips for the B&W look

- Desaturate every clip in CapCut (Adjust → Saturation −100) so the B-roll matches the motion graphics.
- Keep each B-roll shot 2–4 s, then cut back to the graphic that carries the number or key word.
- Show real NASA footage (FLEX-2, Saffire, ISS) at the moments where you name NASA. That earns the "NASA data used" points.
- Under-18 rule: don't use any shot with a recognisable child.
